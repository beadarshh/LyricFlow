LyricFlow - floating synced lyrics for Windows
==============================================

Requires Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit.
No install and no .NET needed: just run LyricFlow.exe.

Getting started
1. Unzip the folder anywhere (for example Documents\LyricFlow) and double-click LyricFlow.exe.
   Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" screen because the app is not code-signed:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. A purple/pink music-note icon appears in the system tray (it may be under the ^ arrow).
3. Play a song in Spotify, YouTube Music in your browser, Apple Music, or most other players.
   The lyrics appear word by word near the bottom of the screen.

Controls
- Ctrl+Alt+L        Show or hide the lyrics, opened in the middle of the screen your mouse is on.
- Tray icon         Right-click: Show lyrics, Move overlay, Settings, Quit.
                    Double-click: open Settings.
- Move overlay      Unlock the overlay, drag it where you like, then untick it to lock it again.
- Full-screen look  In Settings, set Background to Black or Custom and the lyrics fill the whole
                    screen. Double-click the screen or press Esc to hide it again.

Settings (tray icon > Settings)
Style (Kinetic or Classic), font, size, window width, background (Transparent, Black or any
colour), opacity, lyric timing, hotkey, and the Kinetic options (block and word colours,
words per page, tilt, size variety, pop-in).

Good to know
- Lyrics come from the free LRCLIB service (lrclib.net) and are cached on your PC, so the app
  needs an internet connection the first time it plays a song. Not every song has lyrics.
- If lyrics run a little early or late, adjust "Timing (ms)" in Settings.
- It can't draw over games running in exclusive full-screen, or on the Windows lock screen.
- Only one copy runs at a time. Quit from the tray icon.
- Your settings are in %APPDATA%\LyricFlow and the lyric cache in %LOCALAPPDATA%\LyricFlow.
  Delete those folders to reset everything or remove the app's traces.
